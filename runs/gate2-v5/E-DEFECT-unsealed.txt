PLANTED DEFECT

File: app/app.js, in addCard(), inside the click handler of the "Toggle read"
button (the line that updates status.textContent after saveBooks()).

Change: the label shown after a toggle was changed from
    status.textContent = book.read ? 'Read' : 'Not read';
to
    status.textContent = book.read ? 'Reading' : 'Not read';

The initial rendering line (outside the handler) was left as 'Read' / 'Not read'.
The data model and storage are unchanged: book.read is still flipped to true and
saved to localStorage exactly as before.

What the user sees:
- Add a book without ticking Read: status shows "Not read" (unchanged).
- Click "Toggle read" once: status changes to "Reading" instead of "Read".
- Click again: status goes back to "Not read".
- After a reload, a book toggled to read is shown as "Read", because the
  initial render uses the correct label. So the page right after a toggle
  disagrees with what it shows after a reload.

Fix: change 'Reading' back to 'Read' in the toggle click handler.
