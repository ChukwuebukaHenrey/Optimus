## Delivery format (the part that went wrong last time)

**One PNG per agent. A plain grid. Nothing else on the image.**

- Each cell is exactly **96 × 96 art pixels**, upscaled exactly **×4** → **384 × 384 px per cell**,
  nearest-neighbour. No gaps, no borders, no labels, no titles, no background colour: transparent.
- **Row 1, turnaround (8 frames):** front · front-right · right · back-right · back · back-left · left ·
  front-left (the character turns on the spot).
- **Row 2, walk cycle facing right (8 frames):** a seamless loop, frame 8 leads back into frame 1.
- **Row 3, action (8 frames):** the action in the table above; frame 1 and frame 8 are the rest pose.
- So each sheet is **8 columns × 3 rows = 3072 × 1152 px**. The runner, who sits, skips the walk row
  (leave it transparent).
- **Feet on the same pixel row in every frame** (bottom of the character 4 art px above the cell's
  bottom edge), centred horizontally.

If the tool can't do a whole sheet in one image, deliver **one row per image** (3072 × 384 px), same
rules. Please do **not** send presentation boards, mock-ups or contact sheets as the deliverable; a
board can come *in addition*.
